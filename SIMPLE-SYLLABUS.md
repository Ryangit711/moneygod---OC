### Real Data Practice (15 min each day)

Go to **fred.stlouisfed.org** and look up:
- `WRESBAL` — bank reserves
- `RRPONTSYD` — overnight repo
- `WTREGEN` — Treasury balance

Compute: **Net Liq = Reserves − Treasury − Repo**. Write: IN, OUT, or NEUTRAL.

## Your Daily Ritual (From Week 5 Onward)

### Every Morning (15 min)
- [ ] 5‑min pre‑session
- [ ] Check plumbing indicators
- [ ] Mark session times on chart
- [ ] Set alarms for session opens

### During Trading Session (1‑3 hrs)
- [ ] Only trade if your setup triggers
- [ ] Size by liquidity regime
- [ ] If no setup → watch + journal
- [ ] One trade at a time. No revenge. No doubling.

### After Session (10 min)
- [ ] Journal every trade
- [ ] Screenshot chart with entries/exits
- [ ] Write: what worked, what didn't, what to change

### Every Monday (15 min)
- [ ] Full plumbing check
- [ ] Weekly liquidity verdict
- [ ] Review last week's journal
- [ ] Did I follow the commandments?

### First of Every Month (30 min)
- [ ] P&L review
- [ ] Adjust strategy if market changed
- [ ] Read one of: `10-investing`, `11-escape`, `12-who-knows`
- [ ] Update your level on the 10‑level mastery path

> If you trade without doing the pre‑session → you are gambling.
